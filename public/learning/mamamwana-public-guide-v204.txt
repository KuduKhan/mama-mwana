ENGLISH
1. Preparing questions: Write questions ahead of an appointment, and confirm all plans directly with a healthcare professional.
2. Family support: Discuss practical ways family members can support one another through everyday responsibilities.
3. Learning together: Celebrate children’s interests with storytelling, songs and play suited to family preferences.
4. Respecting privacy: Check who can view a device before opening private information. Never share passwords.

KISWAHILI — MAFUNZO YA JUMLA
1. Kuandaa maswali: Andika maswali kabla ya miadi na uthibitishe mipango yote na mtaalamu wa afya.
2. Msaada wa familia: Jadili namna wanafamilia wanavyoweza kusaidiana katika shughuli za kila siku.
3. Kujifunza pamoja: Furahieni hadithi, nyimbo na michezo kulingana na mapendeleo ya familia.
4. Kuheshimu faragha: Hakikisha ni nani anayeweza kuona kifaa kabla ya kufungua taarifa binafsi. Usishiriki nywila.

STATUS
