# MamaMwana — shared child information and Kiswahili review

## Caregiver sharing safeguards in draft
- Only an owner can create an invitation for one child, with explicit guardian-authority attestation.
- The recipient must sign in with the exact invited, verified email before accepting.
- Invitations expire after seven days; accepted read-only access expires after thirty days unless revoked earlier.
- The RPC returns a **minimized** child summary with child-specific growth and vaccination entries. It never broadens the original journey RLS or returns pregnancy notes, care planner records or unverified legacy observations.
- The owner can revoke; the invited caregiver can decline or leave. An audit event records changes.
- These are *code controls*, not proof of parental/guardian legal authority. Identity verification, safety review and Kenya data protection/legal clearance remain release blockers.
- The sharing migration must pass two verified-account tests that cover wrong email, unverified email, cross-child requests, revocation, expiry, replayed versions and denied writes.

## Kiswahili
- The English/Kiswahili selector, navigation, core account labels and contextual review warning are implemented.
- Educational and urgent clinical text is deliberately not machine-translated without clinician and independent linguistic review; English remains in those sections.
- Full professionally reviewed Kiswahili is **NOT COMPLETE**; do not advertise bilingual clinical safety until every patient-facing health string is signed off.
- Test long Kiswahili labels at 320, 360, 390 and 430px, screen readers, focus order and 200% browser zoom.
